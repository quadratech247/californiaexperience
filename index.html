Pacific Coast Highway Roadmap

800+ miles of breathtaking coast, redwood arches, and legendary beach towns.

01

San Francisco & Golden Gate

Start your journey at the famous Golden Gate bridge. Cycle along Fisherman's Wharf, explore the cable car tracks, and catch sunset at Twin Peaks.

02

Monterey & Carmel-by-the-Sea

Stroll through ocean vistas, visit the legendary Monterey Bay Aquarium, drive down the scenic 17-Mile Drive, and stay in Carmel's fairy-tale cottages.

03

Big Sur & Bixby Bridge

Feel the coastal chill as you pass over Bixby Canyon Bridge. Gaze at McWay Falls, a waterfall dropping directly onto a pristine cove beach.

04

Santa Barbara & Coast

Savor coastal wines and Spanish-colonial architecture. Explore beach pathways, palm-lined avenues, and sail out towards the Channel Islands.

05

Los Angeles & Santa Monica

Finish your drive under the ferris wheel of the Santa Monica Pier. Stroll through Venice Beach, capture the Hollywood sign, and watch the waves roll in.
